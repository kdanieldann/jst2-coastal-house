A HOUSE, TWO LAYERS

Space first.
Atmosphere follows.

Start in first person. Walk with WASD, hold Shift to run at about 2.3× walking speed, or press V to follow your PINOC character in third person. The hall and water courtyard each have their own immersive scene.

Walk toward a marked doorway to enter the next space with a short fade. Each room is a separate reconstructed scene with its own walkable area. In room view, the map shows how the spaces connect.

Press B to explore the editable concept plan. Dimensions are in metres, measured between wall centre lines, with 0.16 m thick walls. These are design assumptions, not surveyed dimensions of the generated rooms. The plan has its own structural collision boundaries.

Each room uses one consistent scene so furniture and doorways do not suddenly change as you move. The water courtyard has its own newly generated environment.

Export reference saves a plan camera image and generation settings. Align room adjusts the scene position and orientation in this browser; it does not verify the reconstructed geometry.
